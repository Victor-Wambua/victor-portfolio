About me

I am a technology and nonprofit leader based in Nairobi, Kenya, working where technology, education and community impact meet. Over more than six years I have progressed from IT Assistant to IT & Systems Manager at Ngong Road Children's Foundation (NRCF), a child- and education-focused nonprofit, taking on more strategic and organisational responsibility at each step.

The work spans strengthening children's access to STEM learning through robotics, technology-based space exploration and virtual reality; building and improving the technology behind nonprofit fundraising, including online sponsorship and donation systems; and growing the foundation's digital presence through organic SEO, Google Ads and social media.

Alongside this I lead Salesforce and NRCF's core information systems, work closely with programme teams, leadership and an external development partner, and translate organisational needs into practical technology. I am strongest in stakeholder collaboration, project delivery and clear reporting.

I also build. On Salesforce that means Apex, Lightning Web Components and Flow, released through pull requests and automated checks. Outside it, I build finance tools on Google Apps Script, WordPress themes for clients, and AI automations that turn meetings, inboxes and help-desk boards into tracked work.

  • Independent product · Version 0.1.0, pre-pilot

    Approval Portal

    A payment request and approval application for Google Workspace, built on Google Apps Script. It replaces form-based approval workflows with a role-based dashboard, a durable processing queue and an audit trail.

    Screens come from a local preview that uses fictitious data only.

    Problem
    Payment approvals needed clear roles, a reliable processing queue and a trustworthy audit trail.
    Technology
    • Google Apps Script
    • JavaScript

    • Automated tests

  • IT & Systems Manager, NRCF · In the sandbox

    Stripe and Salesforce integration

    Salesforce takes over everything after a card payment. Each payment closes the right NPSP donation, and the donor gets the right thank-you email without anyone sending it by hand.

    Problem
    Payments arrived in Stripe, but donation records and thank-you emails depended on manual steps that could be missed or doubled.
    Technology
    • Apex
    • Lightning Web Components
    • Salesforce Flow
    • Stripe
    • NPSP
    Design
    A backstop matches each payment by its Stripe ID, then the recurring donation, then the donor's email and amount, and creates a task to verify the match. An admin console switches each of 13 email types between Live, Test and Off, logs every change, and offers a preview and one-click resend.
    Status
    Built and tested in the Salesforce sandbox: 26 of 26 Apex tests pass, with 89–92% coverage on the main classes. Not yet in production.

  • Independent product · Live

    Loan Portal

    A complete loan book on Google Apps Script: interest, late charges, extensions and rollovers, partly funded loans with funder payouts, a daily email summary, and expenses imported from M-Pesa messages.

    Problem
    Personal lending had outgrown a spreadsheet: rules were applied by hand and nobody could see what changed or why.
    Technology
    • Google Apps Script
    • JavaScript
    • GitHub Actions
    • Automated tests
    Design
    Every money rule is written down and numbered before it is coded. Every save is journalled, and a daily check flags any save that did not finish. Changes go through pull requests and deploy automatically.
    Status
    In daily use. Over 930 automated tests run against simulated Google services.
