About Me

I'm a technology and nonprofit leader based in Nairobi, Kenya, working at the intersection of technology, education and community impact. Over more than six years I've progressed from IT Assistant to IT & Systems Manager at Ngong Road Children's Foundation (NRCF), a child- and education-focused nonprofit — taking on increasing strategic and organisational responsibility along the way.

My work spans strengthening children's access to STEM learning through robotics, technology-based space exploration and virtual reality; building and improving the technology behind nonprofit fundraising, including online sponsorship and donor systems; and growing NRCF's digital presence through organic SEO, Google Ads and social media.

Alongside this, I lead Salesforce and our core information systems, work closely with programme teams, leadership and external partners, and translate organisational needs into practical technology solutions — with a particular strength in stakeholder collaboration, project delivery and clear reporting.
