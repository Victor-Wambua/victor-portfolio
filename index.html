About me

I am a technology and nonprofit leader based in Nairobi, Kenya, working where technology, education and community impact meet. Over more than six years I have progressed from IT Assistant to IT & Systems Manager at Ngong Road Children's Foundation (NRCF), a child- and education-focused nonprofit, taking on more strategic and organisational responsibility at each step.

The work spans strengthening children's access to STEM learning through robotics, technology-based space exploration and virtual reality; building and improving the technology behind nonprofit fundraising, including online sponsorship and donation systems; and growing the foundation's digital presence through organic SEO, Google Ads and social media.

Alongside this I lead Salesforce and NRCF's core information systems, work closely with programme teams, leadership and external partners, and translate organisational needs into practical technology. I am strongest in stakeholder collaboration, project delivery and clear reporting.
